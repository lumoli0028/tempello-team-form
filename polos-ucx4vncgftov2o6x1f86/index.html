Ordering from Vistaprint: costs beyond the shirt

Our six salespeople are in the US, Mexico and Spain. Vistaprint prices are per shirt, but delivery adds cost, and it adds more when the team is in several places.

One address per orderVistaprint's help center says an order ships to a single address. Each address would need its own order, and each order pays its own shipping.Source: Vistaprint help article, 2021. Not re-checked at checkout.
Mexico is not on Vistaprint's listThe US site delivers only to the US, Canada and Puerto Rico. Spain has its own Vistaprint site. Mexico has none, so Hector's shirts would need a US address and a forward (extra shipping, time, and possibly customs).Source: Vistaprint help center, updated 17 Jun 2026.
Free shipping needs $100+Vistaprint advertises free economy shipping on orders of $100 or more. One polo at $45–$75 is under that, so a single-shirt order would likely pay for shipping.Economy is the slowest option (as soon as 8 business days). Faster speeds cost more; prices show at checkout.

Spain: Richard would order on Vistaprint's Spain site, whose catalog and prices can differ from the ones above and have not been checked. Possible shortcut: Vistaprint also runs a custom-quote service for large orders to several locations; not checked for six shirts. Not known yet: the actual shipping charge per address, and import costs. Both only appear at checkout.

About the logo on the photos: these are mockups. The logo is shown in full color; on the polos that are embroidered it will be stitched in fewer, flatter thread colors, so the final look will be a little simpler. The sleeve marks (Nike, Adidas) are the maker's, not ours.

Logo method: embroidered = stitched, slightly raised, durable; printed = heat transfer, flat and smooth, any colors. Vistaprint offers one method or the other on these navy polos, not both.
Also checked and set aside: OGIO Onyx (OGIO mark on the hem), Greg Norman Play Dry (shark logo, few reviews), Callaway Core, Nike Golf Crosshatch (S–L out of stock). Prices are Vistaprint's range per shirt: the lower number is for a larger order, the higher for a small one. The price for our exact order is still to be confirmed.
